## Design Context

### Users

Kāhea is for software testers, API designers, and architects of mature agentic workflows. They arrive as technically sophisticated evaluators looking for tools that make AI-assisted delivery more capable, deterministic, functional, and demonstrably tested. The landing page should quickly establish that AI-first tools are the evolution of software tooling and that Kāhea is a credible implementation of that idea.

### Brand Personality

Light, kinetic, and exact. The voice is concise, technically literate, and confident without hype. The emotional progression should move from curiosity, through comprehension, to earned trust.

### Aesthetic Direction

A white, editorial canvas with compelling typography and Framer-style scroll choreography. One continuous particle field should behave like Kāhea's computation, transforming through inspect, plan, grant, invoke, and evidence as the visitor scrolls. The page is a kinetic story rather than a conventional feature-card landing page, ending in a focused path to the GitHub repository. Preserve the existing near-black and rust-red brand palette. Avoid dark cyberpunk styling, generic SaaS grids, decorative gradients, glassmorphism, and motion that is unrelated to the product model.

### Design Principles

1. Make the computation visible: motion must explain Kāhea's deterministic pipeline rather than decorate it.
2. Lead with a belief, prove it with mechanics: introduce the AI-first evolution thesis, then reveal inspect-to-evidence behavior with concrete language.
3. Keep the surface editorial and spare: typography, whitespace, and a single evolving visual system carry the experience.
4. Earn technical trust: every claim should connect to a real Kāhea capability, command, plan, grant, or evidence artifact.
5. Performance and access are part of the aesthetic: target perfect Lighthouse category scores, preserve keyboard and screen-reader meaning, and provide an equally coherent reduced-motion narrative.
